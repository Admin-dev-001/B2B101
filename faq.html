Can a non-resident form a company with B2B101? +
Yes — every jurisdiction we support allows 100% foreign ownership and fully remote formation. You never need to travel to complete the process.
How long does formation actually take? +
On average 48 hours for jurisdictions like Delaware or the UK, and up to 5 business days for Singapore or Malaysia. Your dedicated manager gives you an exact timeline before you commit.
Is a bank account included with formation? +
Not automatically, but we handle account opening as part of the same engagement — most clients have a working account within a few days of incorporation.
When can I get a company card? +
Virtual cards are typically issued the same day your account is approved. Physical cards ship afterward and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.
Which currencies can I hold and transact in? +
USD, EUR, and GBP are supported as core currencies, plus 20+ additional currencies for holding and exchange inside your dashboard.
Do you support VISA, Mastercard, and UnionPay? +
Yes. We can affiliate your business directly with any of the three networks — see the Payment Network Affiliation page for details on the process.
What's the difference between an EMI and a PI license? +
An EMI license lets you issue your own electronic money and provide broader payment services. A PI/SPI license is lighter-weight and focused specifically on remittance and payment processing — better suited to smaller operations.
Can I buy a company that already holds a license? +
Yes — see our Ready-Made Companies page. Acquiring an already-licensed entity can save months compared to a fresh application.
Are there any hidden fees? +
No. Every cost is disclosed upfront before you commit to anything. If your situation requires something outside the standard scope, we'll tell you before any work begins.
How do I stay in touch with my account manager? +
Via email or your preferred messaging channel — whichever you prefer. Your dedicated manager stays with you from the first consultation through ongoing operations.
Do you handle ongoing compliance after formation? +
Yes — annual filings, renewals, and reporting obligations are covered under our ongoing compliance service, so your company stays in good standing year after year.
